{# ── How Reserved works ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

How Reserved works

  1. 1
    Income arrives

    Reserved recognises income from connected financial accounts and other sources.

  2. 2
    Reserved adds context

    The user confirms what the income represents, with support from invoice and accounting information where available.

  3. 3
    Tax is estimated

    Reserved estimates the Income Tax, National Insurance and other liabilities associated with the income.

  4. 4
    Tax reserve updates

    Reserved updates its limited model as entered freelance income changes; this does not establish the total amount due for the tax period.

# Yapily finite completion map

This map prevents an open-ended sequence of slices. Y1 through Y3 are the
finite AIS path. PIS and sweeping VRP are separate October-v1 payment
workstreams whose live activation is conditional on the contractual, provider,
legal, security and assurance gates below. They do not extend or block truthful
completion of the selected AIS-only scope.

| Stage | Finite outcome | Entry gate | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Y1 | Provisional AIS Hosted Consent request contract | Reviewed unsigned draft and supplied current documentation | Complete: network-inert documented GB request boundary and strict GET path only; no provider response, browser handoff, hosted URL or status parsing; focused and repository tests; independent final review passed |
| Y2 | Secret-bearing response boundary, browser handoff, sandbox AIS execution and retained evidence | First establish reviewed URL/custody/redaction/logging controls; then approved sandbox access, confirmed exact provider schema, authorized credential custody and test-user/data controls | Resolve the documented hosted-URL fragment conflict; safely handle create/status responses and browser redirect; complete Hosted Consent, accounts and transactions journeys; retain negative/error/expiry and institution-scoped evidence |
| Y3 | Integration, custody and user-journey hardening after contract/legal delta | Executed/approved AIS terms, legal and provider delta resolved, Y2 accepted | Route/state binding, encrypted consent credential custody, retention/revocation, observability and user-journey assurance without widening Data scope |
| Separate PIS gate | October-v1 Single Payment/PIS capability; default and primary introductory payment journey | Yapily Payments contractual/provider confirmation plus legal, regulatory, security, custody, same-owner destination, consent/status and product approval | Separate scoped implementation and assurance; live activation never inferred from AIS |
| Separate sweeping VRP gate | October-v1 sweeping VRP capability; expressly opt-in and never enabled, preselected or implied by default | Yapily Payments contractual/provider confirmation, verified bank coverage and sweeping eligibility, acceptable commercials, plus legal, regulatory, security, custody, same-owner destination, consent/status and launch-assurance gates | Separate scoped implementation and assurance; live activation never inferred from correspondence, AIS or same ownership |

## Terminal completion gate

The Yapily AIS workstream is complete when Y1, Y2 and Y3 evidence is accepted;
the exact selected accounts/transactions journey is operational within approved
UK institutions; credential, consent, redirect, retention and revocation
controls pass independent review; legal/provider deltas are closed; and release
authority is recorded. Completion of this AIS-only workstream does not require
PIS or VRP, but that finite boundary does not remove either payment capability
from the October-v1 product scope recorded in `FOUNDER_DECISIONS.md`.

## Out of scope

Sub-Clients/sub-applications, non-GB territory, identity, balances and other
unselected Data Products, webhooks not separately documented and authenticated,
bulk payments, deposits, commercial term interpretation, liability conclusions
and regulatory conclusions are outside this map. PIS, VRP, sweeping,
same-owner-transfer controls, pricing confirmation and production activation are
outside the finite AIS Y1-Y3 tranche but remain separately gated October-v1
payment work. New AIS slices require an evidenced gap against the finite gates
above; they must not be created merely to continue the workstream.

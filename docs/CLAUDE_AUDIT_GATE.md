These are indicators rather than a mechanical checklist. The gate should be reassessed whenever material evidence changes.

The objective is sufficient evidence for a responsible, explicitly scoped
launch decision—not proof that no defect or uncertainty can exist. Residual
risks may remain where they are understood, bounded or clearly disclosed,
monitored and proportionate to the supported customer purpose. Deterministic tax
arithmetic remains subject to the independent correctness standard wherever the
relevant facts and rules are known.

The gate need not wait for perfection. It should be opened while Claude can still materially influence launch decisions and remediation, not after all decisions have become costly to change.

## Reassessment rule

At each major gate or material defect, ask whether remaining uncertainty is primarily understood implementation work or discovery work suited to a fresh independent reviewer. Bring the audit forward if a new systemic pattern suggests the builder's model may be incomplete; delay it where known missing foundations prevent meaningful end-to-end assessment; and preserve contingency after audit for fixes and re-review.
